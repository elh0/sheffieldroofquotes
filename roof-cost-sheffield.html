How much does a new roof cost in Sheffield? (2026 guide)

A full re-roof is one of the biggest jobs on a house, and prices vary a lot. These are typical 2026 ranges for Sheffield homes, so you know roughly what to expect before anyone comes out.

Repair: a few slipped or broken tiles£150–£400
Repair: leak around chimney flashing£250–£700
Re-roof: 2-bed terrace (concrete tile)£5,000–£7,000
Re-roof: 3-bed semi (concrete tile)£7,000–£10,000
Re-roof: 3-bed semi (natural slate)£10,000–£15,000
Flat roof: garage or extension (EPDM)£1,500–£3,500
New uPVC fascias, soffits and gutters£1,200–£2,500
Scaffolding (often included)£600–£1,500

Typical 2026 prices for Sheffield, including VAT where charged. Your quote depends on the job.

What changes the price

Roof size and pitch, the material (concrete tile is cheapest, natural slate dearest), access for scaffolding, whether the timbers need work, and how many chimneys, valleys and dormers there are.

Repair or replace?

If the roof is under about 40 years old and the problem is in one spot, a repair is usually the answer. Widespread slipped tiles, sagging, or daylight in the loft point towards a re-roof.

Questions to ask before you sign

Is scaffolding included? Is the old roof stripped and disposed of? What membrane and battens are used? Is there a written guarantee, and is it insurance-backed? When is payment due?

Do I need planning permission?

Not usually for a like-for-like re-roof. Changing the material on a listed building or in a conservation area can need consent, so check with Sheffield City Council if that applies.
