Questions

How quickly will a roofer call?

Usually the same or next working day. For an active leak, pick "Leak or repair" and say so in the notes.

Is it really free?

Yes. We're paid by the roofer we work with, not by you. You only pay for work you agree to.

Will lots of firms call me?

No. Your request goes to one local roofer only.

Do I need scaffolding?

Most work above a single storey does. Scaffolding is usually 15 to 20 percent of a re-roof bill and should be on the quote.
